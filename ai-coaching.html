Starting from absolute zero

Which assistant to use, how to talk to it, and why your first few attempts felt underwhelming. No prerequisites, no shame, no jargon — we go at whatever pace is comfortable.

The tools worth your time

There are thousands. Maybe six matter for you. We work out which ones fit how you actually operate, and I'll tell you plainly which hyped ones to skip.

Prompting that gets real results

Why the same question gets a brilliant answer one day and a useless one the next — and how to write the kind of request that works every time.

An assistant shaped around you

Not a generic chatbot. One that knows your business, your people, your language and your rules, and remembers them between conversations.

Your first working automations

Something that runs without you — an inbox that triages itself, follow-ups that send on their own, a report that builds overnight. We build it together, live, in your accounts.

Documents, data and drudgery

Contracts summarized, spreadsheets cleaned, research compiled, transcripts turned into action items. The work you dread, handed off.

Writing in your own voice

Emails, posts, proposals, listings — drafted to sound like you rather than like a press release. Getting this right is a skill, and it's teachable.

Using AI safely

What's safe to paste and what isn't, how to catch a confident wrong answer, and where to put guardrails so a mistake stays small.
